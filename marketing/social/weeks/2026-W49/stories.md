# Week 49 stories: how to post them (about 2 minutes a day)

The frames are in `stories/` (1080 × 1920). Stories are not posted automatically:
post them from your phone. Save the frames to your phone once (open each file in
GitHub on the phone → ⋯ → Download, or send them to yourself).

Every frame keeps the top and bottom clear for Instagram's own bar and reply box.
Where a frame has a big empty area, a sticker or a shared post goes there.

**Adding a sticker:** Instagram → + → Story → pick the frame from your gallery →
sticker icon (square smiley at the top) → Poll / Questions / Link → drag it into
the empty area.

**Sharing a post on top of a frame:** open the post → paper-plane icon → Add to
story. Then tap the background-image icon, or use the sticker tray → photo
sticker → pick the frame, and pinch so the frame fills the screen behind the
post. If that's fiddly, post the frame first and share the post as the next story.
