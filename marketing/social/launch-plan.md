# Sitetru on Instagram: 30-day launch plan

Goal for the first 30 days: **25 trial sign-ups from Instagram and the first
5 paid projects**, and learn which message makes contractors click. Followers
are not the goal; trials are.

## Before day 1: set up the profile (you do this, about 1 hour)

Accounts have to be created by you, on your phone and email. The agent
never creates accounts or handles passwords.

1. **Instagram**: `@sitetru` (instagram.com/sitetru), switched to a
   **Professional → Business** account, category *Software*.
2. **Facebook Page** "Sitetru", linked to the Instagram account (needed for
   insights, ads and, later, API posting).
3. **Profile**
   - Name field: `Sitetru · Site management app` (the name field is searchable)
   - Bio:
     ```
     Daily logs, labour, materials & RA bills for Indian contractors
     Your foreman updates the site from Telegram
     14-day free trial · no card
     ```
   - Link: `https://sitetru.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio`
   - Profile picture: the Sitetru mark on navy (`#12203F`).
   - Contact button: email and WhatsApp Business number.
4. **Highlights** (covers in brand navy/cobalt): *Start here* · *Telegram* ·
   *Bills & POs* · *Labour* · *Pricing* · *FAQ*.
5. Pin three posts once they exist: the launch reel, "How it works" carousel,
   pricing carousel.

## The funnel

Reel (reach) → profile → link in bio → sitetru.com → 14-day trial →
guided first project (video guides) → ₹99 per project a month.

Two things make or break it:
- **Reply to every comment and DM within a few hours** in launch week.
  "Price?" in a DM is a sale in progress: answer with the price, the trial
  link and an offer of a 10-minute call.
- **Talk to every trial user** on day 2 and day 10. You will learn more from
  ten calls than from any dashboard.

## Cadence

- 5 feed posts a week: 3 reels, 2 carousels.
- Stories daily: behind the scenes, polls ("How do you track labour today?"),
  reposts, "new this week".
- 1 Live or a 30-minute walkthrough on a weekend in week 3.
- Best times to test: 7–9 pm IST weekdays, 10–11 am Sunday.

## Weeks

### Week 1: launch (what it is, who it's for)
- Day 1 reel: launch film (vertical Pulse cut, 40 s): "Built for Indian sites."
- Carousel: "5 signs your site has outgrown WhatsApp groups".
- Reel: Telegram → dashboard: the foreman sends a message, the log appears.
- Carousel: pricing: about ₹3 a day per project, 14-day trial, no card.
- Reel: founder, 30 s, to camera: why you built it. [founder records]

### Week 2: the money problems
- Reel: scan a bill, matched to the PO, the rate difference flagged.
- Carousel: "How RA bills go wrong (and how to never bill a day twice)".
- Reel: stock card: cement in vs used, the missing bags show up.
- Carousel: GST on POs, CGST/SGST vs IGST in one slide each.
- Reel: site engineers don't see your margins.

### Week 3: proof and how-to
- 3 reels cut from the video guides (Daily logs: a whole week in one go;
  Labour: lump sums; Procurement).
- Carousel: "A day on site in Sitetru", morning to evening.
- Live or walkthrough: set up your first project in 15 minutes.
- Ask the first trial users for feedback; with permission, a quote
  (record it in `consents.md`).

### Week 4: convert
- Reel: "What ₹99 a month replaces": the diary, the calls, the spreadsheet.
- Carousel: FAQ: data in India, Telegram, roles, export.
- Reel: a real user's story **only if** they agree (else founder demo).
- Story countdown to the end of the first users' trials, with a call offer.
- Review: which pillar drove trials → plan month 2.

## Paid boost (optional, from week 2)

Only boost posts that already did well organically (saves + shares high).
Start at ₹300–500 a day for 5–7 days, target: men and women 25–55, Tamil
Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra, Telangana; interests: construction,
civil engineering, real estate development, contractors. Measure cost per
trial sign-up; stop anything above ₹400 a trial until the page converts
better. Never more than you approve per week.

## Phase 2: hands-off posting (when you want it)

Instagram allows scheduled posting through Meta's Graph API for a Business
account linked to a Facebook Page. When the drafts are reliably good:
1. You create a Meta developer app and connect the Instagram account.
2. The token is saved as a secret on the cloud environment (a secure form,
   never pasted in chat).
3. The weekly session then schedules the approved posts. Approval stays with
   you: only posts you have marked `approved` in `posts.md` are scheduled.
Until then, use Meta Business Suite's scheduler: upload the week's assets
on Sunday and schedule them in one sitting (about 20 minutes).
