Cancellation & Refund Policy

Version 2026-08-25 · Last updated 25 August 2026

This policy explains how subscription cancellations and refunds work for Sitetru, a service operated by B Gowtham, sole proprietor (“Sitetru”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). It should be read together with our Terms of Service. Sitetru is a subscription software service (SaaS); it does not sell physical goods.

1. Try before you pay

Sitetru offers a permanently free plan and a 14-day free trial of a paid plan so that you can evaluate the Service before making any payment. We encourage you to use the free plan and trial to confirm the Service meets your needs before subscribing.

2. Cancelling your subscription

  • You may cancel at any time from Settings → Plan & Billing in the app, or by contacting us at support@sitetru.com.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for; it is not downgraded mid-period.
  • After the paid period ends, your organization moves to the free plan (or loses paid access), and no further charges are made.

3. Refunds

Because a free plan and a 14-day free trial are available before any charge, subscription fees are generally non-refundable. However, we will review refund requests fairly in the following situations:

  • Billing errors — duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, or charges after a valid cancellation are refunded in full.
  • Failed provisioning — if a successful payment did not unlock the plan or add-on you paid for and we cannot resolve it, we refund that charge.
  • Discretionary requests — if you wish to request a refund for another reason, email us within 7 days of the charge. Approved refunds are at our reasonable discretion.

Add-on purchases (for example, additional project slots) are non-refundable once the capacity has been provisioned. Applicable taxes (such as GST) are refunded only to the extent permitted by law.

4. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through our payment gateway (Razorpay). Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days; the time for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@sitetru.com from the email address on your account, with your organization name and the payment reference. You can also reach us via our Contact page. We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days.

This policy applies to purchases made directly through the Sitetru app. Enterprise agreements with separate written terms are governed by those terms.
