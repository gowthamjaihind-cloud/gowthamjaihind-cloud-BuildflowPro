Shipping & Delivery Policy

Version 2026-08-25 · Last updated 25 August 2026

Sitetru, a service operated by B Gowtham (sole proprietor), is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) product. It is delivered entirely online, and no physical goods are shipped.

1. Digital delivery

Access to Sitetru is provided electronically through our web and mobile applications and, optionally, the Sitetru Telegram bot. There is no physical shipment, and no shipping or delivery charges apply.

2. When access is granted

The free plan is available immediately on sign-up. When you purchase a paid plan or add-on, access is provisioned electronically once payment is confirmed by our payment gateway (Razorpay) — ordinarily within a few minutes. If a successful payment has not unlocked your plan, please contact us and we will resolve it promptly.

3. Service availability

Once activated, the Service is available over the internet on a continuing basis for the duration of your subscription, subject to our Terms of Service.

4. Questions

For anything related to access or delivery, reach us via our Contact page. Cancellations and refunds are covered by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
