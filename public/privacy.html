This Privacy Policy explains how Sitetru (“Sitetru”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Sitetru web and mobile applications, the Sitetru Telegram bot, and related services (the “Service”). It applies to personal data we process as a business, and describes the choices and rights available to you. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Sitetru operates the Service and determines how personal data submitted by account holders is processed for the purpose of running the Service. Where your organization uploads data about its own staff, vendors, or projects, your organization is responsible for that data and Sitetru processes it on the organization’s behalf. For questions or requests, contact privacy@sitetru.com.

3. How we use information

  • to provide, operate, secure, and maintain the Service and its multi-tenant isolation;
  • to authenticate you and manage organization membership and roles;
  • to provide features you use, including procurement, cost, scheduling, reporting, and AI-assisted document reading and insights;
  • to send transactional messages such as invitations and account notices;
  • to meter AI usage and enforce plan quotas, trials, and subscriptions;
  • to detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security, and technical issues;
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

Our legal bases (where applicable) include performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, your consent (which you may withdraw), and compliance with law.

4. AI processing

When you use AI features (for example, scanning a vendor invoice or generating project insights), the relevant content is sent to a third-party AI provider (currently Google Gemini) to produce a result that is returned to the Service. We send only the content needed for the feature. AI output may be inaccurate and should be reviewed before use. We do not use your Customer Data to train our own models.

6. International transfers & storage

The Service is built on Google Cloud infrastructure, and some processing (including certain Cloud Functions in the Singapore region) and sub-processors may be located outside your country. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we rely on the safeguards offered by these providers and applicable law.

7. Data retention

We keep Customer Data for as long as your organization’s account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, or an Owner deletes an organization, we delete the associated data from our live systems, except where we must retain limited records to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Backups and logs are retained for a limited period and then expire. Aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained.

8. Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where relevant, the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Sitetru builds two of these directly into the app:

  • Export — from Settings → Privacy & Data → Export my data, you can download a machine-readable (JSON) copy of your profile and, if you are an Owner or Admin, your organization’s data.
  • Delete — from Settings → Privacy & Data, you can delete your account, and an Owner can delete an entire organization and its data.

You can also make a request by emailing privacy@sitetru.com; we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. If a request concerns data your organization controls, we may direct you to, or coordinate with, your organization.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect data, including per-tenant access controls enforced by database security rules, authentication through a trusted identity provider, encryption in transit, and server-side handling of secrets and API keys. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the version and date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data-subject requests, or complaints, contact us at privacy@sitetru.com.
